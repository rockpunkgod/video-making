# First-person solving cinema: incremental internal workflow

> Scope: the historical Reading D cinema lane and its bounded techniques. Current R06 FREE_SHOWCASE starts at the [current production workflow](workflows/CURRENT_PRODUCTION_WORKFLOW.md). This page does not impose a full two-pass structure, transfer old approval, or provide the default R06 authoring input.

Reuse the frozen source, approved locating trace, native word objects and two-pass picture. The editorial layer may change delivery and disclose existing reviewed evidence; it cannot create a second method, grammar partition or answer permission.

`ae/experiments/reading-d-dual-pass/cinema.py` validates source-pointer hashes, native event bindings, unique audience beats and exact AI scratch-copy approval. This is deterministic provenance lint, not semantic truth certification. Human experience and language reviews remain independent.

Each beat records its current viewer goal, attended object IDs, reading task, source/event references and separate action, attention and comprehension frame clocks. Guided replay must explain a decision omitted by Fast Pass. Clock allocations are editorial intentions, not measurements of a viewer's comprehension.

Generate new narration from an explicitly reviewed scratch script with the current replaceable producer. Keep exact transcripts, individual WAV hashes and fresh PENDING listening status. Resample to 48 kHz PCM16 and pad to frame boundaries through the existing voice-delivery interface. Do not cut or speed speech to fit pictures.

`fit_voice` adds duplicated frames only inside approved settled holds. Verify adjacent RGB content before extending a hold. Preserve the original native-frame sequence. When timing changes, regenerate captions, picture mapping, native AE remap keys, Premiere placement and receipts together.

`cinema.jsx` builds a new 4K24 AE derivative in an isolated copy. It retains the original source comp and word objects, hides obsolete silent-preview metadata and adds source-bound captions. Never overwrite accepted projects or infer new audience approval from object readback.

Resolve imported Premiere items by actual absolute media path, never by duplicate display names. Create a fresh native sequence and place narration with explicit seconds converted from versioned frame coordinates. Inspect a complete live timeline before export. `premiere_production/cinema.py` verifies native placement and actual encoded waveform correlation. Bind an installed Adobe encoding preset explicitly when automatic discovery fails.

Keep source narration, ducked music and tactile SFX stems. The current registered bridge creates three audio tracks and has no track-add operation; stereo beds occupy two tracks. The minimal-SFX version therefore uses a derived music/SFX bed while individual narration remains editable. Original stems and their hashes remain available. Independent native SFX-track editing is a remaining adapter capability gap.

After a mutation times out, reconcile against fresh host state before continuing. A mismatched or unavailable readback remains unresolved. Missing coordinate conversion, shifted/truncated speech and incomplete timeline pagination are rejected before an export can be accepted.

Guided copy uses normal task-specific prompts: direct attention to the relevant condition, compare the actual samples and check the displayed qualifier. Convey scope through the concrete source and check; avoid generic defensive warnings. Updated copy requires new transcript-bound takes, captions, timing and pending human reviews. Stationary reading has a null Action Clock; concrete movement windows reference the native event table. Attention and comprehension allocations remain editorial intentions.

Research and content reviews remain independent. Independent transfer belongs to Method Validation or future Paid Mastery, and is not a prerequisite for an ordinary Free Showcase episode. Current-case teaching claims, actual displayed-source/recording rights, final editorial content and actual exported media must pass the versioned [Free Showcase policy](../FREE_SHOWCASE_POLICY.md). Cinematic engagement, guided understanding and human listening keep their actual separate status; internal review files do not confer publication permission. No fresh transfer exercise is invented when upstream authority is absent.

Real scripts, original questions, media, projects and receipts stay in ignored local data. Commit only reusable workflow, synthetic tests and sanitized acceptance evidence.
