# First-person solving cinema: incremental internal workflow

Reuse the frozen source, approved locating trace, native word objects and two-pass picture. The editorial layer may change delivery and disclose existing reviewed evidence; it cannot create a second method, grammar partition or answer permission.

`ae/experiments/reading-d-dual-pass/cinema.py` validates source-pointer hashes, native event bindings, unique audience beats and exact AI scratch-copy approval. This is deterministic provenance lint, not semantic truth certification. Human experience and language reviews remain independent.

Each beat records its current viewer goal, attended object IDs, reading task, source/event references and separate action, attention and comprehension frame clocks. Guided replay must explain a decision omitted by Fast Pass. Clock allocations are editorial intentions, not measurements of a viewer's comprehension.

Generate new narration from an explicitly reviewed scratch script with the current replaceable producer. Keep exact transcripts, individual WAV hashes and fresh PENDING listening status. Resample to 48 kHz PCM16 and pad to frame boundaries through the existing voice-delivery interface. Do not cut or speed speech to fit pictures.

`fit_voice` adds duplicated frames only inside approved settled holds. Verify adjacent RGB content before extending a hold. Preserve the original native-frame sequence. When timing changes, regenerate captions, picture mapping, native AE remap keys, Premiere placement and receipts together.

`cinema.jsx` builds a new 4K24 AE derivative in an isolated copy. It retains the original source comp and word objects, hides obsolete silent-preview metadata and adds source-bound captions. Never overwrite accepted projects or infer new audience approval from object readback.

Keep Premiere clips, narration, ducked music and optional minimal tactile SFX separately editable. Importing an XML or finding an audio stream is insufficient: inspect actual native timeline paths, complete speech placement and final encoded waveform correlation. A silent native export is a failed take. A separately assembled playable proof must be labelled as such; it does not certify the native Premiere export.

Release gates remain independent: cinematic engagement, guided understanding, independent transfer, human listening, teaching certification, actual displayed-source rights and final editorial approval. No fresh transfer exercise is invented when upstream authority is absent. Internal review files do not confer publication permission.

Real scripts, original questions, media, projects and receipts stay in ignored local data. Commit only reusable workflow, synthetic tests and sanitized acceptance evidence.
