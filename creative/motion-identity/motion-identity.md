# Swiss Cognitive Motion · v0.1

This is a visual mnemonic registry, not an ANSWER teaching registry. It supplies
metaphors, movement phases, effects and memory phrases. Triggers, conclusions,
candidate exclusions, evidence and Stop Rules always come from the frozen package.
No method specification is evidence that the method is valid on a given question.

The shared grammar is Magnetic Alignment, Evidence Develop, Focus Breathing,
Semantic Carry, Micro Lock and Boundary Seal. Fog Blue `#7E96A8` develops literal
evidence; Soft Brick is reserved for an explicitly bound discrepancy. No bounce,
neon highlighter, red cross, forced beat sync, or whole-screen object reset.
Carry the same logical object across a Logic Match Cut. Name the method after
the spatial action settles, as small editorial metadata.

These are movement principles, not fixed logo clips. Text length and supplied
evidence may change the geometry and timing. G01 must retain the extracted blank
as the route origin and close at the supplied boundary. It cannot invent other
branches, imply excluded alternatives are wrong, or supply a final word form.
F01 must retain all candidates not explicitly excluded upstream. R06 negative
space must remain empty. R09 cannot invent a summary during compression.

Each Shot Plan stores `signature_motion.method_id`, `signature_id`, timed phases,
`brand_motion`, the carry object and the two registry hashes. A registry change
requires a new visual revision. A method label incompatible with the frozen
claim bindings is rejected. Deterministic structural lint is not proof that
the animation explains a method successfully.

Structural effects need a semantic reason. Depth treatments are grouped by
purpose, with at most two simultaneous treatments; accents at most one. Motion
must settle into readable states. A declared HOLD is checked against changing
keyframe values and reveals. Native frame review remains necessary for overlap,
contrast, typography, unintended motion and visual fidelity.

## Brand recognition critique

Review signature recognizability, method-motion fidelity, brand grammar
consistency, mnemonic clarity, distinctiveness and teaching alignment. Reject
a proposed signature if merely swapping the method name leaves the same spatial
logic equally appropriate. Reject a series if its six methods lack a common
motion grammar. These judgments are pending human review, not automatic PASS.

Future internal memory protocol: show a 2–4s signature excerpt with method
name/ID hidden; ask which method it suggests and why. Record participant,
excerpt SHA, response, confidence and recognizable phase. Do not supply the
answer beforehand. No learner recognition or retention result is claimed in
v0.1. Only the protocol is reserved.

G01 human review questions: Does the motion convey a method? Is Extract → Route
→ Seal memorable? Is it richer than the old version without becoming busy?
Does it still feel Swiss Cinematic Editorial? Is the seal memorable? Does the
motion help understanding? Would seeing it again suggest “先判空位”?

Full G01, F01, R02 and V10 remain blocked until a named human provides
`APPROVED_DIRECTION` bound to the current render contract (including final
copy and visual hashes). Approval of direction does not grant teaching,
source, editorial or music release.

## Quiet Intellectual Elegance

Use one continuous Chopin solo-piano bed. Op.15 No.3 is the current internal
starting choice, then Op.32 No.1, Op.55 No.1 and Op.72 No.1. Voice dominates,
structural SFX are sparse, and music recedes for English evidence and Stop.
Slow gain ramps and near silence belong in Premiere, not AE. No BPM matching,
recommendation engine, stems, music generation, or automatic composition.

The recording manifest stores title, composer, performer, recording source,
license, license evidence and file SHA. Composition identity cannot establish
recording permission. User-provided audio without proven recording permission
is `SCRATCH_MUSIC`, `Music License HOLD`, `public_use_allowed: false`.
Numbers are a starting point; they cannot pass human listening review.

The five listening questions are: does music compete with voice; does it add
texture; does it feel pretentious; are Evidence/Stop transitions natural;
does removing it lose atmosphere? Store actual human answers. Freeze the
basic music direction once acceptable; stop further music-system development.
