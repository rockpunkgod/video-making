## Problem and final direction

The original acquisition had four concrete weaknesses:

| Area | Weakness | Response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Attention | The selected evidence appeared before a visible search process | Establish the question target, then the complete supplied passage before reading |
| Camera and space | A direct move to an already known sentence looked like an answer reveal | Enter at the first real source line; follow actual line order to the existing span |
| Motion timing | Highlight, movement and confirmation competed in one event | Settle the camera before the brief filled highlight; clarify the whole condition afterwards |
| Information | Persistent headings, range lines and an entire question felt like teacher annotation | Keep only the exact original task cue during reading; remove visible guides and step labels |

The first synthetic A/B was actually delivered. The user's response was
“进入完整原文扫，不是直接定到那一句”. It is recorded as `NEEDS_REVISION`, not PASS.
The final steering defines first-person problem solving: the picture represents
information retained in mind and current attention. It must not look like a
teacher annotating the page.

Astra provided the creative recommendation and inspected native preview/lens
calibration frames. Root reviewed the boundaries and implemented it. The complete
question appears first. Its original keyword object stays quietly at the same
reading position; other stem fragments and options leave view. The complete
original passage appears, then native camera focus carries the reading point
from the first paragraph. After the hit settles, a short filled highlight occurs.
The complete existing evidence sentence lifts using its original word objects,
while the task cue exits and the remaining source retreats into a separate band.

There are no underlines, range marks, visible scanning cursor, arrows, step
labels, rewritten mental summary or persistent lecture heading. This is a
creative representation, not a claim to measure gaze, memory or reading speed.
